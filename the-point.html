01 — The idea
One decisive move, at the point where a little moves a lot.

Every episode answers one question: where is the leverage? The identity is built to feel like that answer, direct, weighty, no hedging. The mark is a beam at the instant it tips. The recurring device is a single red dot, “the point”: it is the pivot in the mark, the period in the logo, and the marker on every episode number. One idea you can spot at a glance and can’t confuse with anyone else.
